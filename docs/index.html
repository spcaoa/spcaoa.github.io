Where we stand

Running Palm Court costs about ₹30.1 L a month. Maintenance brings in ₹19.2 L. Interest covers some of the rest. The reserves cover the remainder. These pages show the numbers, what the Managing Committee is doing, and what happens next.

In six sentences

  1. Last year the association spent ₹48 lakh more than it collected. The difference came from the reserves. The General Body was not asked.
  2. This year is running the same way. April to July: ₹1.20 Cr spent, ₹76.8 L billed.
  3. The reserves are about ₹9.33 Cr. That money is for big repairs in the years ahead. Its interest is already being spent on running costs.
  4. A resident Sub-Committee reviewed last year's accounts. Their questions have gone to the previous Managing Committee and to the auditor. The answers will be published here.
  5. A Special General Body Meeting on 18–19 October will decide the budget and the maintenance rate. The 1 October bill stays at the current rate. Only the General Body can change it.
  6. Until then the gap is met from the reserves. The amount drawn is published here every month.
