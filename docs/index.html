Where we stand

Running Palm Court costs about ₹29.9 L a month. Maintenance brings in ₹19.2 L. The rest has been coming out of the reserves — the money owners paid at handover, meant for big repairs. That cannot go on. These pages show the numbers, what the Managing Committee is proposing, and what you will be asked to decide.

The short version

  1. Between April and August we spent ₹1.50 Cr. Maintenance for those five months was ₹96.1 L. The difference came from the reserves.
  2. The reserves are about ₹9.69 Cr. Their interest already goes on running costs. Last year ₹50 L of the reserves themselves was used as well, and this year another ₹38 L so far.
  3. The Managing Committee is proposing a 23% increase in maintenance, with a second option that adds a one-time ₹1 lakh per flat to rebuild the reserves. The options page shows what each would mean for your flat.
  4. A Special General Body Meeting on Sunday, 25 October discusses it. Every owner then votes in an online poll that opens straight after the meeting and closes a week later.
  5. The 1 October bill is at the current rate. Only the General Body can change it.
  6. Last year's accounts were reviewed by the Finance Sub-Committee, a group of resident owners. Their report, and the previous Managing Committee's answers, go out with the meeting notice. The ₹48 lakh shortfall last year was reported at the July AGM.
