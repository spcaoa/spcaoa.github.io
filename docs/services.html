Service performance · published monthly from October 2026

Lifts and complaints

The two things residents asked for most in the survey were lifts that work and complaints that get closed. Both will be measured and published here every month, by the 10th, so that the trend is visible to everyone and not just to the committee.

Lift uptime

Lifts were the lowest-rated service in the survey (3.30 of 5) and the top priority for 54% of households. The association pays about ₹16 L a year for the lift maintenance contract and spent ₹6.75 L on batteries last year.

No data published yet. The first monthly lift report will be published here by 10 October 2026, covering September. It will show, for each wing: the number of breakdowns, the hours each lift was out of service, the uptime percentage, and the time taken to restore service, from the lift contractor's call log checked against the security register.

Complaint resolution

Half of the households that answered the survey had raised a complaint in the last six months. They found it easy to report (3.90 of 5) and hard to get closed: ownership until closure scored 3.05, and time to resolve 3.13.

No data published yet. From October, a monthly table from MyGate: complaints opened and closed by category, how many were still open at month-end, and the average days to close. The standard the committee has set is: acknowledged within four working hours, an update every 48 hours, closed only when the resident confirms.

How this will be kept honest

  1. Lift figures come from the contractor's call log, cross-checked against the security gate register of technician visits.
  2. Complaint figures come straight from MyGate's ticket export; nothing is re-typed.
  3. Both are published by the 10th of the following month, with the raw file available on request to any owner.
