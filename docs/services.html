Lifts and complaints

Lifts and complaints were the top two issues in the survey. Both are measured here, and published every month from October.

Complaint resolution

In the survey, half the households had raised a complaint in the last six months. Reporting was easy (3.90 of 5); getting it closed was not — ownership scored 3.05, time to resolve 3.13. Below is the actual record from MyGate for 2 Jan 2026 to 21 Sep 2026, unedited.

The picture the numbers give is not the one the survey describes, and both are true. Most tickets are dealt with quickly: half are closed inside 2.6 days and 75% inside a week. But 23 took longer than a month, and 9 had to be reopened. It is the tail that people remember, and the average of 8.2 days is dragged up by it. Only 30 of 336 tickets were rated, averaging 4.13 of 5.
