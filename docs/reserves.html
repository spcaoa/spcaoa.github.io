How ₹11.76 crore became ₹9.33 Cr

Jun 2022
₹11.76 Cr collected by Sobha at handover. For sixteen months Sobha ran the estate from it: ₹1.88 Cr charged, ₹1.17 Cr of interest credited.
Nov 2023 – Jun 2024
Sobha returned ₹9.30 Cr in nine instalments. It went into fixed deposits at HDFC and ICICI. Sobha kept ₹91 L as security for its facility service.
2024–25 and 2025–26
Interest was spent on running costs. In 2025–26, ₹50 L of principal was also used, without General Body approval.
Today
₹9.33 Cr, of which ₹8.58 Cr is in bank deposits. A full bridge from the audited accounts will be in the meeting pack.

Going to the meeting
