How the model works

  1. Spending: ₹26 L a month on the committee's base (April–July actual, ex lumps), plus ₹8 L for the lift contract in June and December and the quarterly tax instalment; or ₹33 L flat on the Treasurer's base. Savings reduce it from January 2027.
  2. Income in a quiet month: ₹1.8 L of late maintenance, ₹0.8 L rentals and fees, ₹1.9 L HDFC interest, ₹0.5 L Sobha interest. In a billing month (October, January, April, July): 92% of the quarter's bills at the rate then in force.
  3. GST collected is passed to the government; it is not income. If input credit is on, the association keeps back its own vendor GST in proportion to the share of billing that is taxable.
  4. Option 2's contribution nets ₹69.5 L after GST, collected in November. The Sub-Committee's 10.1% is applied to the bill with GST carved out of it, as their Annexure C did; the other options add GST on top of the base.
  5. The yearly view adds ICICI interest (≈₹38 L accruing) and Sobha interest, and deducts tax on all interest, to show whether an option balances a full year.
