How the model works

  1. Spending: ₹26 L a month, plus ₹8 L for the lift contract in June and December, plus tax each quarter. Or ₹33 L flat, the Treasurer's figure. Savings reduce it from January 2027.
  2. Income in a quiet month: ₹1.8 L late maintenance, ₹0.8 L rentals and fees, ₹1.9 L HDFC interest, ₹0.5 L Sobha interest. In a billing month: 92% of the quarter's bills.
  3. GST collected goes to the government. If input credit is on, the association keeps its own vendor GST in proportion to taxable billing.
  4. Option 2 nets ₹69.5 L after GST, collected in November. The Sub-Committee's 10.1% is applied to the bill with GST inside it, as in their report.
  5. Next year adds ICICI interest (₹38 L) and deducts tax on all interest.
